# Prompt Recipes

Formula: `[subject] + [setting] + [style] + [lighting] + [camera/composition] + [aspect ratio] + [what to avoid]`
Always end with: `no watermark, no extra text, no borders`.

## Reel / Story background (vertical 9:16)
Abstract [topic] background, [color palette] gradient, soft glowing shapes, cinematic lighting, clean empty center area for text overlay, vertical 9:16, no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## YouTube thumbnail (horizontal 16:9)
[Subject] with [expression/action], bold high-contrast colors, dramatic rim lighting, shallow depth of field, subject on the right third with empty space on the left for a title, horizontal 16:9, no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## Instagram post / carousel (portrait 4:5)
[Subject] in [setting], modern minimal style, [brand colors], soft natural light, centered composition with generous margins, portrait 4:5, no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## Product shot
Studio product photo of [product] on [surface], [background color] seamless backdrop, soft box lighting with gentle reflections, 85mm lens, sharp focus, [aspect ratio], no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## B-roll still
Photorealistic scene of [action] in [place], [time of day] lighting, documentary style, 35mm lens, natural colors, [aspect ratio], no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## Icon / logo mark (square 1:1)
Flat vector icon of [object], simple geometric shapes, [2-3 colors], solid [color] background, centered, square 1:1, no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## Illustration
[Subject] illustrated in [style: flat vector / 3D clay / anime / watercolor], [color palette], [mood], [composition], [aspect ratio], no watermark, no extra text, no borders

## Notes
- Arabic text: leave it out of the image and add it in the edit.
- Short English text: put it in quotes, e.g. `with the title "AI NEWS"`.
- Fix-up message (send once only): "Make it [brighter / more minimal / change the background to X], keep everything else the same."
